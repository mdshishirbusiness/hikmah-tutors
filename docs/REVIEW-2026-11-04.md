# Site review, 4 Nov 2026

A page-by-page walk of the site on invented data, as three people: a new visitor on a phone, a signed-in tutor, and
the owner (phone and laptop). Each finding says how it was checked. Items marked **(owner)** came from the owner's
message; the rest were found in the walk.

## A. Broken now (fix first)

1. **The phone menu doesn't open properly.** It opens only 40 px tall, so its links (including **Tutor log in**) can't be
   seen. Cause: the header's glass blur makes the menu size itself to the header. Measured in the browser at 375 px.
2. **Signed-out visitors have no Log in button on the phone header.** It sits only inside the broken menu. Together with
   item 1, a tutor on a phone can't find how to log in. **(owner)**
3. **Quick Pad area search misses areas.** "kaz" finds nothing (Kazla, Kazihata), and "upo" finds Alupotti but not
   Uposhohor. Cause: the search lowercases what you type but compares it with capitalised names. Tested in the browser.
4. **The logo glitch.** The header, footer and admin icon is a crop of the full logo, so cut-off pieces of the "H", "T"
   and the "RAJSHAHI" pill stick out on the right edge. **(owner)** Answer to "should H and T be there?": not in the small
   icon. The small icon should be the mark alone (figure, cap, star), with the name in real type beside it, as the header
   already does. The full logo should show both letters whole; in the source picture the swoosh also clips the left side
   of the H and T. Plan: redraw the mark as a clean SVG (sharp at every size, a few KB) and use it everywhere.
5. **First sign-in page shows "RUET · RUET".** The university and institution repeat. Seen on `/me/setup`.

## B. New tutors can't join on the site **(owner)**

6. **There is no registration.** A new tutor who signs in with Google lands on "Profile not found", a dead end with no next
   step. Nothing on the site links to the old Google Form either. How it works step 1 assumes the tutor already filled
   that form. Checked in code (`me.tsx`, `auth.server.ts`) and on every page.
7. **Proposed flow.** Tuition board → **Become a tutor** (header, footer, board, tuition page) → Continue with Google →
   a 4-step form on one page:
   - **About you:** name, phone, gender, photo.
   - **Study:** university, department, session, HSC group.
   - **Teaching:** areas, classes, subjects, lowest salary, transport.
   - **Confirm:** availability and terms.

   The tutor gets a T-code and status **Waiting for check**, and can browse but not apply until the owner approves. The
   owner gets a **New tutors** queue in Admin (photo, details, Approve or Ask to fix). After that, close the Google Form.
8. **First sign-in has no Subjects step**, but match % weighs subjects the most (2 of 4.5 points). New tutors would always
   score low. Subjects belong in the registration form (item 7) and in setup.

## C. Loading and motion **(owner)**

9. **No feedback while a page loads.** Nothing shows after a tap until the next page arrives, so on a slow phone it
   feels stuck and people tap again. Checked: no site-wide loading state exists.
10. **Proposed loader** (like Towfique HSC English, which uses a pulsing logo, the name and a sliding bar):
    - A thin emerald bar across the top starts on every tap.
    - If the page takes longer than about 0.4 s, the logo mark and a sliding bar fade in at the centre.
    - Fast pages never flash the overlay.
    - It respects reduced motion.
    - Note: Towfique's bar slides left to right; tell me if you really want right to left.

## D. Forms: Quick Pad, guardian request and New tuition **(owner)**

11. **There are three separate forms for the same request**, each built differently:
    - Quick Pad: old styling with emoji, outside the admin layout. I didn't redesign it last round, and should have.
    - Guardian request form: a native dropdown of 70 areas, plus an "other area" box that is always visible.
    - New tuition: the new chip form.

    Proposal: **one shared set of fields**. Quick Pad keeps its offline saving and its order (phone first), but uses the
    new design and gains the fields a tuition needs (time of day, session length, tutor from RU/RUET/Medical, start). Then
    "Post" turns a lead into a tuition with no retyping. The guardian form uses the same pieces with fewer fields, in
    plain guardian language.
12. **"Other" is a dead end.** Class "Other" (Quick Pad, request form, setup) has nowhere to write what it means, and
    subjects have no "Other" at all. **(owner)** Proposal: picking Other opens a short text box ("e.g. Hifz, IELTS, Class 5
    scholarship"). It is saved as written and shown on the post. Insights lists the most common "Other" entries, so a
    frequent one can become a real chip.
13. **As the lists grow** (70+ areas, 18 subjects, 19 classes), long chip walls get slow to scan. **(owner)** Proposal:
    - Classes are grouped in rows (Junior, SSC, HSC) as on first sign-in.
    - Areas use one search-as-you-type picker (Bangla or English) with the top 12 as chips and "Use '<what you typed>'"
      for a new one.
    - The same picker is used on every form.

## E. Owner tools **(owner)**

14. **Act as a tutor.** Possible and useful for support, with guard rails:
    - Start from the tutor's admin page.
    - A mango banner stays on every page: "Acting as T-1234 · Exit".
    - It ends after 30 minutes, and every change made is logged as the owner, on the tutor's behalf.
    - Blocked while acting: accepting terms, confirming a tuition, opening a guardian's number, and logging out the
      tutor's own sessions.

    Fixing details (name, areas, subjects) already works from Admin → Tutor without acting as them.
15. **Referral commission (20%).** The Refer page exists; it has no reward yet. Proposal:
    - A referral is **Pending** until the tuition is confirmed and the placed tutor has paid the media fee.
    - It then becomes **Earned** (20% of the agreed monthly salary), then **Paid** when the owner marks it paid by bKash.
    - The tutor sees all three on the Refer page; the owner sees a Referrals list in Admin.

    Questions below.

## F. Design polish

16. **Tuition board cards are tall on a phone** (about 480 px each, so about 1.5 cards per screen). A tighter card would
    show 2.5 per screen: salary and area big, other facts on one line, the requirement as one line.
17. **On the tuition page, the Apply button scrolls away.** A sticky Apply bar at the bottom of the phone screen keeps it
    in reach while the tutor reads the fee terms.
18. **Public pages open with a dark forest band** (board hero, How it works, Request a tutor), the same tone as the admin
    sidebar. If this is the "admin bar tone" you meant, the public pages can move to cream with forest type and one mango
    or emerald accent band. Admin keeps the forest.
19. **"For you" shows to signed-out visitors.** It should read "For you · Log in", or appear only after log in.
20. **A post can contradict itself** (e.g. "Male tutor" chip with requirement text "Female tutor"). The publish check
    could warn when the requirement text names the other gender.
